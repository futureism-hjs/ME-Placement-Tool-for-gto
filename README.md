`ME Placement Tool for gto` is a standalone GregTech Odyssey adaptation of ME Placement Tool `2.1.4` for Minecraft `1.20.1`, Forge `47.4.20`, GTOCore `0.5.6-beta`, GTCEu `26.7.3`, and the pack's customized AE2 `15.267.4` runtime.

It keeps the upstream `meplacementtool` namespace and does not depend on GTOHJS.

## Included Content

- `meplacementtool:me_placement_tool`: places blocks, AE2 parts, facades, and source fluids from a linked ME network.
- `meplacementtool:multiblock_placement_tool`: bulk placement with selectable counts, direction modes, previews, and one-step undo.
- `meplacementtool:me_cable_placement_tool`: line, plane-fill, and branching cable placement with five AE2 cable families and color control.
- `meplacementtool:prism_core` and `meplacementtool:key_of_spectrum`: crafting material and cable-tool color upgrade.
- Original models, textures, cable UI, radial menus, HUD, key bindings, Chinese/English/Japanese language resources, and five GTO-native crafting recipes.
